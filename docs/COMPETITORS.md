# Competitive Landscape

## Direct Competitors

### ChartAI
- **What:** Mobile app for AI chart analysis
- **Pricing:** Freemium + IAP
- **Strengths:** Good UX, multi-exchange support
- **Weaknesses:** No Telegram, no paper trading, no education
- **Our move:** Telegram-native + paper trading

### TrendSpider
- **What:** Pro-grade technical analysis platform
- **Pricing:** $54-321/mo
- **Strengths:** Deep indicators, automation
- **Weaknesses:** Expensive, steep curve, no AI-vision
- **Our move:** Beginner-first, $0, "5-second glance"

### NexisQ
- **What:** AI assistant for traders (text input)
- **Pricing:** Freemium
- **Weaknesses:** Requires trading jargon, no vision
- **Our move:** Zero-friction, screenshot only

## Indirect Competitors

- TradingView — charting, but not AI analysis
- ChatGPT / Claude — can analyze charts, but no trading UX
- Telegram signal groups — noise, no education, often scams

## Market Position

We sit at the intersection of:
- AI sophistication
- Telegram-native delivery
- Beginner-first UX
- Zero cost
- Multilingual (ru / en / it)

Competitors ship EN-only. We launch with 3 languages.

## Defensibility (Moat)

1. **Data flywheel** — every screenshot/outcome pair improves the prompt
2. **Community** — paper trading leaderboard creates switching cost
3. **Speed** — 5 seconds from screenshot to answer
4. **Zero-friction** — no signup, no install, no payment
5. **Multilingual** — ru / en / it at launch
